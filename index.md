# Inventory Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Inventory Manager is a desktop application for managing inventory and eBay
listings. For privacy questions, contact stp2011@zoho.com.

## Information the app uses

If you connect an eBay account, Inventory Manager uses eBay OAuth to access
the seller account information needed for the features you choose. This may
include business policies and, when you use listing features, listing details
such as SKU, title, description, photos, price, quantity, category, condition,
and inventory location.

## How information is stored and shared

Inventory Manager runs on your computer. It stores inventory and listing
records in a local SQLite database. The eBay Client Secret and refresh token
are stored in your operating system's credential store. Short-lived access
tokens are held temporarily in memory.

When you connect to eBay or use eBay features, the app sends the relevant
requests and information directly to eBay. Inventory Manager does not operate
a separate server for this data and does not sell it.

## Retention and removal

Your local inventory data remains on your computer until you remove it from
the app or delete its database. To stop eBay access, revoke Inventory Manager
in your eBay account's authorized app settings. You can also remove its saved
credentials from your operating system's credential store.

## Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data use changes.
